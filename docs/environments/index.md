# Environments

Gym.NET registers 39 environment ids in four families: classic control, Box2D, toy text and MuJoCo. The gallery plays
24 of them as the showreel recorded them. Select a cartridge to watch it on the screen, with the episode's seed, steps
and return, and the call that makes it.
